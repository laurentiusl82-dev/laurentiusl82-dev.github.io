Short version

Zombie Meter has no account, no server, no ads and no tracking. Your measurements are processed on your phone and stay there. The developer does not receive, see, sell or share them.

Who is responsible

Zombie Meter is made by Laurentiu Scortanu, an independent developer based in Romania, who is the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for the little personal data the developer may receive (see "If you write to us" below). Contact: lsldevapps@gmail.com

Camera

The front camera is used only during the eye scan, and only after you allow it. Frames are analysed live on your phone by Google ML Kit face detection to estimate eye openness, blinks and head movement. No photo or video is saved, uploaded or shared, and no face template or biometric identifier is created or kept — only summary numbers such as "eyes open 82% of the time". You can refuse or withdraw camera access at any time in your phone settings and simply skip the eye scan.

Google ML Kit

Face detection is done by Google ML Kit on the device; camera images are never sent to Google. According to Google, ML Kit may send limited technical data to Google (such as device model, app and SDK version and performance metrics) to maintain and improve the service, under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Motion sensors

The accelerometer is used only during the balance test to measure how much your body sways. The raw data is discarded right after the test; only summary numbers are kept.

What is stored on your phone

Numeric results (for example reaction times, errors, sway, eye openness), the date of each check, your optional answers before a check (hours slept, number of coffees, how you feel), badges, settings, your language choice and the date and version of the terms you accepted. They are kept in the app's private storage on this device, are not linked to your name or any account, and stay until you delete them.

Why it is processed (legal basis)

On-device processing happens only so that the features you choose to use work (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)). The optional check-in answers and the camera are used only with your consent (art. 6(1)(a) and, to the extent eye signals could reveal health information, art. 9(2)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time by skipping them or deleting your data. Because this data never leaves your phone, the developer cannot access it.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your phone. No push service or server is involved, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Sharing your result

If you tap "Share as image", an image of your result card is created on your phone and passed only to the app you choose. If you tap "Challenge a friend" or "Share my week", a text is shared with a link to a page on laurentiusl82-dev.github.io (hosted by GitHub Pages). A challenge link contains only your reaction time in milliseconds, your level and the challenge date — no name and no identifier. When someone opens the page, GitHub may log technical data such as the IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement); the page itself uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. What happens next to anything you share is governed by the app you share it with.

If you write to us

If you email us, your email address and message are used only to answer you (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)) and are deleted within one year after the conversation ends, unless the law requires keeping them longer.

What is NOT done

No analytics, no advertising, no profiling, no automated decisions with legal effects, no selling or sharing of data with third parties and no transfer of your measurements outside your phone.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to and port your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Data on your phone can be deleted with Settings → "Delete all my data" or by uninstalling the app. For data the developer holds (emails), write to lsldevapps@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro) or to the authority in your own country.

Backups and security

Device backups made by your phone may include the app's storage under your own Google or Apple account. Protect your phone with a screen lock, because anyone who can open the app can see your results.
